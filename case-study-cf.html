Case Study 01 | VilCart | Meta Ads

₹2 Lakh Ad Spend → 8,000 Leads → 90 C&F Partners → ₹9 Crore Business Value

A C&F partner acquisition campaign across Karnataka that connected paid media performance with partner conversions and confirmed revenue.

8,000Leads generated

Overview

VilCart operates across FMCG, rural commerce, distribution, and B2B markets. The company wanted to expand its distribution network by acquiring suitable C&F partners across Karnataka.

Between January and May 2026, I planned and managed a Meta lead-generation campaign that generated 8,000 leads from ₹2 lakh in advertising spend. Ninety leads converted into C&F partners, generating ₹9 crore in confirmed revenue directly attributed to the campaign.

Project Information

  • Client: VilCart
  • Industry: FMCG, rural commerce, distribution and B2B
  • Campaign period: January to May 2026
  • Market: Karnataka
  • Primary platform: Meta Ads

Challenge

VilCart needed to identify potential C&F partners across a broad regional market. The campaign had to reach relevant business prospects at scale while maintaining an efficient cost per lead.

The goal was not simply to generate inquiries. Leads needed to enter the sales and follow-up process and progress into partner conversions that could be connected to measurable business value.

Objective

Generate qualified C&F partner leads across Karnataka and convert suitable prospects into VilCart distribution partners.

  • Expand the C&F distribution network.
  • Generate relevant business leads at scale.
  • Maintain efficient acquisition costs.
  • Support conversion from lead to confirmed partner.

Strategy and Execution

I created a targeted Meta lead-generation strategy focused on potential C&F partners in Karnataka. The approach combined location targeting, business-focused messaging, relevant creatives, Meta Lead Forms, and ongoing performance optimization.

  • Defined target markets and the ideal C&F partner profile.
  • Planned campaign messaging and developed ad creatives.
  • Launched Facebook and Instagram lead-generation campaigns.
  • Tested targeting and creative variations.
  • Monitored lead volume, CPL, and lead quality.
  • Passed leads into sales and WhatsApp follow-up processes.
  • Tracked C&F partner conversions and revenue impact.

Lead generation

8,000 leads were generated from ₹2 lakh in advertising spend, producing an average CPL of ₹25.

Partner conversion

90 prospects converted into C&F partners, representing a 1.13% lead-to-partner conversion rate.

Revenue impact

Confirmed revenue generated from the 90 conversions was ₹9 crore, directly attributed to this Meta campaign.

Results

  • ₹2 lakh total advertising spend
  • 8,000 leads generated
  • ₹25 average cost per lead
  • 90 C&F partners converted
  • ₹10 lakh average ticket value per conversion
  • ₹9 crore confirmed revenue
  • 1.13% lead-to-partner conversion rate
  • 45× revenue ROAS

My Role

I planned the performance marketing strategy, managed Meta advertising campaigns, developed audience and location targeting, planned messaging and creative direction, monitored lead quality, optimized performance, analyzed results, and supported the C&F lead acquisition funnel.

The campaign demonstrates the complete journey from paid media to lead generation, sales conversion, partner acquisition, and revenue impact.
