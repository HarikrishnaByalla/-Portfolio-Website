Case Study 02 | Regal India Tours | B2B Lead Generation

B2B Lead Generation Campaign for Employee Transportation Services

A multi-location acquisition campaign that generated business enquiries for employee transportation services across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and other markets.

₹5 lakhTotal campaign budget
3,020Total leads generated
76Recorded conversions
2.52%Overall conversion rate

Overview

Regal India Tours wanted to generate B2B enquiries and acquire corporate clients for its employee transportation services across multiple locations.

I managed a multi-location lead-generation campaign using Google Ads and email marketing. The campaign focused on reaching businesses that may require employee transportation solutions, generating enquiries, and nurturing prospects through the conversion process.

Project Information

  • Client: Regal India Tours
  • Industry: B2B travel and employee transportation services
  • Campaign duration: 4 months
  • Markets: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and other locations
  • Channels: Google Ads and email marketing

Challenge

Regal India Tours needed to generate a high volume of B2B enquiries across multiple markets. The campaign also needed to support the movement of relevant prospects from initial enquiry through follow-up and conversion.

Objective

The objective was to generate B2B leads and acquire corporate clients for employee transportation services across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and other target locations.

Strategy

I used a multi-location B2B acquisition strategy combining Google Ads and email marketing. The approach was designed to create a consistent flow of business enquiries while supporting lead nurturing and conversion.

  • Focused on multiple B2B business markets.
  • Reached companies interested in employee transportation services.
  • Generated enquiries through paid search.
  • Used email marketing to support lead nurturing and follow-up.
  • Monitored lead volume, CPL, and conversion performance.

Campaign Execution

  1. Identified priority locations and B2B market opportunities.
  2. Planned the multi-location lead-generation approach.
  3. Ran Google Ads campaigns for employee transportation enquiries.
  4. Used email marketing to support lead nurturing.
  5. Monitored lead volume and acquisition cost.
  6. Evaluated conversion performance across the campaign period.
  7. Measured final lead and conversion outcomes.

Lead generation

3,020 B2B leads were generated over a four-month campaign period.

Acquisition efficiency

The campaign delivered an average cost per lead of ₹165.56 from a ₹5 lakh budget.

Conversions

76 recorded conversions were generated at an average spend per conversion of ₹6,579.

Results

  • ₹5,00,000 total campaign budget
  • 3,020 total leads
  • 76 recorded conversions
  • 2.52% overall conversion rate
  • ₹165.56 average cost per lead
  • ₹6,579 average spend per conversion

My Role

I managed the digital marketing and lead-generation campaign activity across multiple locations, monitored performance, supported lead nurturing, and contributed to the process of moving B2B prospects toward conversion.
