Overview

Within a three-month period, I built, deployed, and SEO-optimized three websites for different business and content purposes: TrueFold.in, VilCartFoundation.org, and RuralCEO.com.

The project combined web development, content architecture, technical SEO, on-page optimization, and deployment. Each website was structured around its audience, content model, and future growth requirements.

Project Information
