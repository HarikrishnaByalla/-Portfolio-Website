Built a Custom SEO & Blog Dashboard to Streamline Content and SEO Operations

A centralized frontend and backend system that helped the SEO and content team manage website updates, publishing workflows, and ongoing content operations.

Overview

I developed a custom SEO and blog management dashboard to centralize website SEO activities, content operations, keyword management, and blog publishing workflows.

The dashboard enabled the SEO and content team to manage routine website changes from a centralized platform instead of depending on developers for every update. It combined SEO controls with a content management workflow so the team could manage page optimization and blog publishing in one system.

Project Information

  • Project type: Custom SEO and blog management dashboard
  • Users: SEO and content team
  • Development: Frontend and backend
  • Positioning: SEO automation, CMS, and marketing technology

Challenge

Routine SEO and content updates can become slow when every change requires developer involvement, code modifications, or redeployment.

The team needed a more efficient way to manage SEO information, update website content, organize keywords, create blogs, and publish content through a structured workflow.

Objective

The objective was to build a centralized platform that allowed the SEO and content teams to manage website optimization and blog operations more independently.

  • Streamline repetitive SEO tasks.
  • Centralize content operations.
  • Support on-page SEO changes.
  • Provide a structured blog publishing workflow.
  • Reduce developer dependency for routine updates.

SEO Management

  • SEO title management.
  • Meta description management.
  • URL and slug management.
  • Image and alt-text management.
  • Page content editing.
  • Keyword management and tracking.
  • Content categorization.
  • Internal linking management.
  • SEO performance monitoring.

Blog Management

  • Create and edit blog posts.
  • Manage drafts and publishing workflows.
  • Organize content using categories and tags.
  • Manage featured images and content details.
  • Maintain a structured public blog library.
  • Support ongoing team content operations.

The team wrote nearly 150 blogs using the broader content management workflow.

Results and Impact

  • SEO and blog management centralized in one platform.
  • Routine SEO changes could be managed without developer support.
  • Structured workflow for creating, organizing, and publishing blogs.
  • Content operations supported nearly 150 blogs written by the team.
  • Frontend and backend workflows brought together for marketing operations.

My Role

I handled SEO strategy and requirements, dashboard planning, SEO architecture, CMS and content workflow planning, keyword and content structure, frontend and backend development, technical SEO implementation, testing, and optimization.
